Click to play
WASD move, Space jump or swim up
Hold left click: mine blocks. Click: hit animals
Walk over dropped items to pick them up
Right click: place blocks, eat, shear sheep, fish, open crafting table
E: inventory, crafting and armor. Q: drop an item
Keys 1-9 or mouse wheel: pick a slot (9 slots, 64 per stack)
Hold Shift: sprint. Hold Down arrow: crouch

Xbox controller: plug it in, press any button, then press A to start.
Left stick move, right stick look, A jump, B crouch, RT mine or attack, LT place or use, LB and RB change slot, X drop, Y inventory, click the left stick to sprint, Start to pause. In menus use the D-pad and A. On this screen: A or Start resumes, View (Back) saves and quits.
